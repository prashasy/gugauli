Who we are

A collective of farmers in Bundelkhand

Gugauli Organic Producer Company Limited is a collective of farmers in Bundelkhand who grow using organic practices. By pooling our produce, our buying, and our knowledge, we help our members reach better markets and earn a fair price — without the middleman.

What we do

From field to fair price

🌾

Organic Produce

We grow pulses, wheat, oilseeds and vegetables using organic methods — no synthetic pesticides, no harmful inputs.

🤝

Fair Market Access

Collective selling means better bargaining power and fairer prices — we cut out the middlemen who eat into farmer margins.

🌱

Inputs & Training

Bulk-purchased organic seeds, bio-fertilisers, and bio-pesticides for members, plus practical training on organic practices.

📦

Processing & Packaging

Grading, cleaning, drying and packaging — adding value to produce before it reaches buyers and institutions.

Why a Producer Company?

Owned by farmers. Run by farmers.

A Farmer Producer Company (FPC) is registered under the Companies Act, 2013 — but owned entirely by its farmer-members. Each member is a shareholder. Decisions are made together. Profits return to the members who earn them.

  • ✓No middleman markup
  • ✓Collective bargaining power
  • ✓Bulk input buying — cheaper inputs
  • ✓Shared processing & storage
  • ✓Training & technical support
  • ✓Legal identity — GST, bank account
