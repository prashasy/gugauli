For farmers

Grow with us

A farmer producer company owned by its members — you sell together, buy together, and grow together.

Why join

Benefits of being a member

As a farmer-member of Gugauli Organic Producer Company, you become a shareholder in a collective that works for you — not a middleman.

💰

Better prices

Collective selling means we negotiate from strength. Pooled produce reaches larger buyers who pay fairly — not the mandi price set by others.

🛒

Cheaper inputs

Bulk purchasing of organic seeds, bio-fertilisers, and bio-pesticides reduces your input cost significantly compared to buying alone.

📚

Training & support

Practical training on organic farming practices, soil health, pest management, and best practices — at no extra cost to members.

🏛️

Government schemes

As a registered FPC, we can apply collectively for NABARD, SFAC, and state government support schemes that individual farmers often cannot access.

📦

Shared infrastructure

Shared grading, drying, and packaging services — reducing post-harvest losses and adding value to your produce before it reaches the buyer.

🤝

Ownership & voice

You are a shareholder. You participate in decisions. Profits made by the company return to its farmer-members — not to outside investors.

Membership

How to become a member

Joining is straightforward. Here is how it works:

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Call us, send a WhatsApp message, or fill the contact form. Tell us where you farm and what you grow.

  2. 02

    Meet the team

    We will schedule a meeting — in person in Banda or over a call — to explain the membership process, your rights, and what to expect.

  3. 03

    Join as a shareholder-member

    Complete the membership formalities, take up your share, and become a co-owner of Gugauli Organic Producer Company Limited.
